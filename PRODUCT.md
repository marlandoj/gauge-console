# Gauge Console

## Register

product

## Users

Solo builders and Hackyard visitors trying a task-complexity classifier on a laptop. They should understand one result in seconds and inspect its reasoning without leaving the screen.

## Product Purpose

Make the pinned Gauge classifier tangible: enter a task, see its tier and fired reason codes, and inspect the deterministic resolver mapping. Classification runs locally. Harness selection is metadata, never execution or a model recommendation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, candid, approachable. An instrument for inspecting a heuristic, with no invented confidence or performance claims.

## Anti-references

No tiled analytics dashboard, neon command center, navigation sidebar, marketing hero, decorative status feeds, or fake model activity.

## Design Principles

- One working view with input, result and evidence together.
- Explain the result in plain language; preserve exact reason codes for inspection.
- Distinguish abstention, pending input and engine failure from a classified tier.
- Make experiments immediate with examples, keyboard access and local evaluation.
- Keep experimental limitations visible and metrics tied to their actual evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, labeled native controls, visible keyboard focus, textual alternatives to the dial, reduced motion and responsive reflow. Formal accessibility certification is outside this milestone.
